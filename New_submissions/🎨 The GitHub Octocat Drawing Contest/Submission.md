🐙 The GitHub Octocat Drawing Contest
The GitHub Octocat Drawing Contest is an exciting creative competition that invites the community to redesign and reinvent the platform's iconic mascot, the Octocat (the famous creature combining a cat with octopus tentacles).

✨ Key Event Features
Total Creative Freedom: Participants are encouraged to bring any original version they can imagine to life. Popular or suggested themes include:

Cyberpunk Octocat (futuristic and neon style).

Chef-o-cat (culinary version).

Any other completely unique idea straight from the artist's imagination!

Inspiration: Contestants can explore the official GitHub Octodex to see all the thematic variants the mascot has adopted over the years and spark their creativity.

Open Medium: Artists can use any preferred artistic medium, whether it's traditional pencil and paper, a digital tablet and stylus, or a paintbrush and canvas.

Prizes: The most creative and original designs win free credits to redeem for exclusive merchandise (swag) at the official GitHub Shop.
